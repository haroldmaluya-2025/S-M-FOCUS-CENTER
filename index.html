01 / OUR PURPOSE

Learning should not depend on how far you live from the city.

S&M Study & Working Room was created for the young people and working community of northern Cebu. We provide dependable internet, laptops and comfortable spaces where people can study practical skills, complete their work and connect to wider opportunities.
